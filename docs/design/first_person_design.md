# Lethemoor first-person design: locked picks (Duff, Nov 19 2026)

First person is decided (see CLAUDE.md). These answers settle the eight gaps from the Nov 19
brainstorm. Where this file and older docs disagree, this file wins.

## 1. Loop between nights
- The map reshuffles every night, so every run is new.
- Dawn = a lamp-lit safe room: spend salvage, then pick tomorrow's door.
- You keep only what's in your satchel.
- A ferryman sells oil for teeth (or eyes) taken from the dead. He trades, he never attacks.
- Each night offers routes: short and dark, long and lit, or one with a reward.
- A night journal writes itself, and its pages unlock the next district.

## 2. Progression (no skill tree)
- Satchel slots are real finds, never level-ups.
- Lantern upgrades: wider glass, slower burn, a silent shutter.
- Scars: each death leaves a permanent quirk, good and bad.
- Keys open shortcuts that stay open on later nights.
- Hunter's marks: the way you kill a horror becomes your known way to kill that kind.

## 3. Inventory
- Opening the satchel costs a turn. The camera tilts down into the bag: a 3x3 grid of real objects.
- Items rot: wet matches fail, bread molds.
- Quick slot by swiping the lantern hand. Hold to rummage (better pick, more noise).

## 4. Your corpse (revenant)
- It rises 1 to 5 doors away from where you died, and it hunts you.
- It carries YOUR lantern, lit. Kill it and the oil comes back.
- It walks your last route, hums your low-dread tune, sees only by light (hood to hide from it).

## 5. Enemies
- Stalker: veiled bride. Frozen when lit, moves 2 in the dark, dress drags audibly.
- Thrall: faceless dock workers, wet, trailing ropes.
- Choir: three figures that move only while your back is turned.
- Hollow (later): a breathing door; passing through costs dread.
- Ferryman is NOT an enemy (see 1).

## 6. Sound (all yes)
- Stereo: a thrall behind you scrapes in one ear before you see it.
- Heartbeat rises with dread. Footsteps change with floor (stone, water, wood).
- Silent bell: everything goes quiet one turn before the Stalker moves.
- Your corpse breathes, faintly, somewhere on the map.

## 7. Story delivery (all yes)
- Letters nailed to walls, readable only under BRIGHT light (reading costs oil).
- Notes from your dead past selves, handwriting worse each time.
- Voices from drains, one sentence of town history per night.
- The safe room's lamp keeper talks each dawn, less human every time.
- Wallpaper patterns form words from the right spot.

## 8. Web cut "One Night" / the slice
- Goal: reach the bell tower and ring it before the oil runs out.
- Graded at the end: oil left + letters found.

## Open: weapons, traps, bosses (Duff's rules, Nov 19)
- Weapons are used alongside the lantern, found (not bought), and degrade with use.
- Trap setups exist for tougher enemies.
- Bosses always die to a trap, or to a quest for their weakness (silver weapon, a herb).
- Ideas under discussion with Duff; not locked yet.
