# Harbor Alley Review Checklist

Use this checklist after opening the prototype in Godot and on an actual target device.

## Scene and layout
- [ ] Camera is at first-person eye height.
- [ ] Doorway dimensions align to the grid.
- [ ] Wall corners and door lintels are structurally plausible.
- [ ] Floor seams do not form a repeating obvious checker.
- [ ] Props do not block the movement grid unexpectedly.

## Lighting and atmosphere
- [ ] Lantern light is warm but not blown out.
- [ ] Darkness remains readable, not uniformly black.
- [ ] Fog does not obscure the interactable threshold.
- [ ] Enemy silhouette reads at low brightness.
- [ ] Shadow artifacts and light flicker are absent.

## Mobile
- [ ] Test at low and high supported resolutions.
- [ ] Frame pacing is acceptable while turning.
- [ ] Shadow cost is acceptable.
- [ ] Material count and transparency remain restrained.
- [ ] Touch UI and camera framing are not affected by prototype assets.

## Art
- [ ] Stone has scale variation without noisy micro-detail.
- [ ] Wet highlights are broken and subtle.
- [ ] Props support the harbor story.
- [ ] No accidental texture stretching.
- [ ] Palette remains consistent across the alley.
