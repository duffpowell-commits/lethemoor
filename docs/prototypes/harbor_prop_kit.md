# Harbor Prop Kit Study

This standalone procedural scene demonstrates four reusable visual prop families:

- Timber wall brace / structural beam.
- Mooring bollard.
- Slatted crate.
- Lantern post and housing.

## Rules

- No gameplay integration, collision or simulation changes.
- Lantern housing is non-emissive and does not replace gameplay lantern illumination.
- Keep geometry and material complexity modest for GL Compatibility/mobile.
- Rework dimensions, silhouettes and material response after visual review in Godot 4.5.

## Validation required

Open `scenes/prototypes/harbor_prop_kit.tscn` in Godot 4.5. Inspect clipping, camera framing, shadow quality and mobile performance. This prototype has not been engine-validated.
