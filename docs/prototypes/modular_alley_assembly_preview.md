# Modular Alley Assembly Preview

A self-contained composition assembled from repeatable wall segments, a far portal, a side opening, a few story props, lantern and faceless enemy placeholder. The preview owns one floor, one camera, one environment and one key light.

## Scene
Open `scenes/prototypes/modular_alley_assembly_preview.tscn`.

## Intended review
- Wall segments and relief repeat consistently.
- Side gap breaks the corridor's symmetry.
- Lantern sits on a wall and casts a warm point-light pool.
- Portal and thrall provide a distant focal point.
- Center walking path remains uncluttered.

## Caveats
This is still blockout geometry and the script has not been executed in Godot. Verify fog properties and geometry on the target GL Compatibility renderer before treating it as validated. Provisional scale must be compared against the gameplay grid.
